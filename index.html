About

Human-centered strategy and research

I am a Staff Program Manager + Researcher at Google DeepMind where I drive strategic research engagements for Gemini. These days, I'm interested in advanced reasoning, multimodal, and cognitive evals, intersection of systems + LLMs, and human- centered LLMs. I'm based in Mountain View, California.

Previously, I was one of the founding members of Google DeepMind India and helped set-up a world-class research lab. My research has been recognized through awards at leading HCI conferences such as CHI and CSCW.

I graduated with a Ph.D in Computer Science at City, University of London, advised by Alex Taylor (now at University of Edinburgh) and Anoop Sinha (Google). My Ph.D. was generously fully-funded by Google, and I finished the program while being a full-time employee at Google. I hold a Masters in International Business from the Indian Institute of Foreign Trade in Delhi and received my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at DA-IICT, Gandhinagar. I interned at eBay prior to joining Google.

Research

Human-AI collaboration

My research is broadly in Human-AI collaboration. I am currently examining foundational questions to progress towards human-centered LLMs. My past research explored Human-AI collaboration in resource-constrained environments such as public health and education.

Service: COLM '25, UIST '25, DIS '25 (AC), CHI ('19–'26), CSCW ('19–'24), CHIWORK (PC), NeurIPS (2021).

Publications

Selected recent work

Below is a list of my recent publications. * indicates equal contribution.

Aligning Multimodal Reasoning Systems for Human-Centered AI *

CHI 2025 · Divy Thakkar*, Alex Taylor*, Anoop Sinha

Presents a framework for evaluating multimodal reasoning systems against human-centered criteria for reliability, explainability, and co-creation.

Field Deployments of LLM copilots in Public Health

CSCW 2024 · Divy Thakkar, Radhika Arora, Kiran Suri

Longitudinal study showing how LLM copilots augment frontline public health teams in resource-constrained settings.

Best Paper Honorable Mention

Evaluating Cognitive Load in Multimodal Tutoring Agents

CHIWORK 2023 · Divy Thakkar, Jamie Lin, Rahul Sanyal

Introduces cognitive evaluation probes that capture how students interact with multimodal tutoring agents in blended classrooms.
