Divy Thakkar

Staff Program Manager + Researcher at Google DeepMind. Currently building the next interaction and collaboration paradigm for advanced AI systems. Based in Mountain View, California.

About

  • Focused on strategic research engagements for Gemini and making it the best collaborative platform for users.
  • Founding member of Google DeepMind India, helped setup a world-class research lab
  • Research recognized through awards at CHI and CSCW
  • Education: Ph.D in Computer Science at City St.Georges (advised by Alex Taylor (now at University of Edinburgh) and Anoop Sinha (Google)), MBA from Indian Institute of Foreign Trade, Delhi, Bachelors in Computer Science from Dhirubhai Ambani University

Research

Broadly in Human-AI collaboration. Currently examining foundational questions to progress towards human-centered LLMs. Past research explored Human-AI collaboration in resource-constrained environments such as public health and education.
